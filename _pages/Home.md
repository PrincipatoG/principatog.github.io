I'm a CIFRE PhD student in statistics and machine learning at <a href='https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/en/' target='_blank'>Laboratoire de Mathématiques d'Orsay (LMO)</a>, in collaboration with <a href='https://www.edf.fr/en' target='_blank'>Electricity of France (EDF)</a> and <a href='https://inria.fr/en' target='_blank'>INRIA</a>, as part of the <a href='https://project.inria.fr/inriaedf/le-defi/' target='_blank'>Défi EDF-INRIA</a>. My research is supervised by <a href='https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/fr/perso/jean-michel-poggi/' target='_blank'>Jean-Michel Poggi</a>, <a href='https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/fr/perso/gilles-stoltz/' target='_blank'>Gilles Stoltz</a>, <a href='https://www.yvenn-amara.com/' target='_blank'>Yvenn Amara-Ouali</a>, <a href='https://www.imo.universite-paris-saclay.fr/~yannig.goude/about.html' target='_blank'>Yannig Goude</a>, and Bachir Hamrouche.

My research is centered on <strong>uncertainty quantification</strong> and <strong>predictive modeling</strong>, with applications in areas such as <strong>energy forecasting</strong>, including EV charging and load consumption. I am particularly interested in <strong>conformal prediction</strong>, <strong>time series forecasting</strong>, and <strong>hierarchical forecasting methods</strong>.
